# Cite Checker Skill

## When to Use

- Before filing or submitting a brief that contains case citations
- Verifying citations generated by AI tools
- Checking whether an unpublished opinion exists in PACER
- Identifying RECAP docket entries for cited orders and memoranda
- Routine audit of citations in legal documents

## Audience and Work Shape

Audience: US litigation lawyers, paralegals, and litigation-support staff preparing or auditing filings that contain case citations. Not intended for unsupervised drafting, for use by non-lawyers acting on their own behalf, or for jurisdictions other than US federal and state court citations.

Work shape: Pattern-Matched Review. Each extracted citation is matched against an external reference (CourtListener / RECAP). The output is a per-citation diagnostic record for human review. It is not a sign-off, an editorial pass, or a substitute for Shepard's / KeyCite authority checking.

## Scope and Legal Use

This skill provides legal *support*, not legal advice. A `Verified` result means "this volume/reporter/page (or neutral citation) returned a matching opinion record in CourtListener" — it does not mean the case stands for the proposition cited, that the citation is correctly formatted under the relevant court's local rules, or that the brief is fit to file.

Three legal failure modes to recognise:

1. **Legal advice vs. legal support.** The diagnostic report is a research artefact. It does not advise on whether to cite a case, whether the case remains good law, or how to characterise its holding. Treat the output as input to lawyer judgment, not as a substitute for it.
2. **Privilege and confidentiality (CourtListener API).** Running this skill sends extracted citation text — and, where fuzzy search is invoked, surrounding context such as party names and case captions — to a US public case-law API operated by a third party (Free Law Project / CourtListener). Draft briefs, unfiled work product, and any document containing client-identifying or matter-sensitive content are presumptively privileged or confidential. Do not invoke on such material unless: (a) the responsible lawyer has confirmed that routing the cited extracts through a public case-law API is acceptable for this matter; and (b) the skill is configured to send citation strings only, not the surrounding paragraph or quoted passage, wherever the lookup can succeed without that context. When in doubt, redact to citation strings before running, or do not run.
3. **Accountability gap.** A "Verified" label does not transfer accountability for the citation from the lawyer to the tool. The filing lawyer remains responsible under court rules and professional-conduct duties (including Rule 11 and equivalents) for every citation in a submission, regardless of whether this skill returned `Verified`.

## How It Works

### Step 1: Citation Extraction

Parse the document and extract all case citations in standard formats:
- Volume, reporter, page number (e.g., 123 F.3d 456)
- Case names
- Year
- Court (if identifiable from citation format)

### Step 2: CourtListener Lookup

Query CourtListener API with each citation:

1. **Exact match first** — Citation Lookup API
2. **Fuzzy search** — if exact match fails, search opinions
3. **RECAP docket search** — for PACER-only documents

### Step 3: Confidence Scoring

Rate each citation:
- **Verified** — Exact match found, citation is valid
- **Likely valid** — Fuzzy match suggests validity, minor formatting issue
- **Not found** — Citation not found in any database
- **PACER only** — Document exists in PACER/RECAP but not in public databases

### Step 4: Diagnostic Report

For each citation, return:
- Citation as provided
- CourtListener URL if found
- Confidence score
- Specific mismatch explanation if applicable (wrong court, wrong year, etc.)

The report header must explicitly state: **"Verification status — for lawyer review before filing."** It must not state, imply, or be summarised as "fit to file," "cleared," or "approved for filing."

## Confidence Bands

The four scoring labels carry the following required human action:

- **Verified** — exact match in CourtListener. Required action: lawyer still confirms each citation before filing (see pre-filing sign-off gate below). `Verified` is not a green light to file unattended.
- **Likely valid** — fuzzy match only. Required action: lawyer must open the matched opinion, confirm volume/reporter/page/court/year, and confirm the case is the one intended before relying on it.
- **Not found** — no match. Required action: stop. Do not treat as cleared. Lawyer must independently verify (Westlaw / Lexis / authoritative reporter) or remove the citation.
- **PACER only** — docket exists in PACER/RECAP, opinion text may be partial or absent. Required action: lawyer must retrieve the full document from PACER and confirm the cited proposition and pinpoint against the source.

## Pre-Filing Lawyer Sign-Off Gate

Before any document audited by this skill is filed or submitted:

- The output document or report must carry the line **"Verification status — for lawyer review before filing"** at the top.
- A qualified, jurisdictionally-admitted lawyer responsible for the filing must review each citation, including those marked `Verified`, and confirm the citation as used.
- This skill does not authorize unsupervised filing. A `Verified` row in the report is necessary but not sufficient.
- If the lawyer cannot personally confirm a citation, route to escalation (below).

## Out of Scope

In scope: US federal and state court citations recognised by CourtListener (volume/reporter/page and neutral-citation variants), and PACER/RECAP docket references.

Out of scope:
- Non-US citations (UK, EU, Singapore, Australia, Canada, etc.). On detection, halt that citation and route to a jurisdiction-appropriate verifier.
- Statutes, regulations, administrative rulings, and secondary sources — this skill verifies case citations only.
- Shepard's / KeyCite-style validity (whether the case is still good law, has been overruled, distinguished, criticised).
- Proposition-support check (whether the case stands for what the brief says it stands for).
- Bluebook / local-rule citation-format conformance.
- Quotation accuracy against the underlying opinion text (the skill matches the citation, not the quoted passage).
- Privilege or confidentiality classification of the input document — the user is responsible for whether the document is safe to send to a public API.

## Escalation

Stop and route to the responsible lawyer when:

- Any citation returns `Not found` or `PACER only` — do not clear; require lawyer confirmation against the authoritative source.
- A `Likely valid` result has a mismatched court, year, or party name — flag specifically and require lawyer reconciliation before clearing.
- The CourtListener API errors, times out, rate-limits, or otherwise fails to return — do not silently mark citations as cleared; mark them as `Unverified — API failure` and require lawyer review.
- The input contains non-US citations or is identified as out-of-scope — halt those citations and route to a jurisdictionally appropriate skill.
- The input appears to contain client-identifying detail, draft work product, or other privileged content and the lawyer has not separately approved external lookup — halt and request approval before running.
- Conflicting signals (e.g., reporter matches but year is wrong; case name matches but court is wrong) — do not auto-rank as `Verified`; downgrade and require lawyer review.

## Limitations

- Does not verify that the cited case actually stands for the proposition claimed
- Does not check Shepard's or other authority-check services
- PACER-only documents may not have full text available
- Some unpublished opinions may not be digitized
